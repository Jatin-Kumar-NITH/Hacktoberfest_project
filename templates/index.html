                                          About Zenkai

                                          An open-source student assistant built for a hackathon.

                                          The problem

                                          Students get dozens of circulars, hostel notices and exam announcements every semester. They are long, formal and easy to misread, and missing one deadline can mean a fine, a cancelled room or a blocked admit card.

                                          What Zenkai does

                                          It turns any notice into a plain summary, the dates that matter, the documents to bring and a checklist, and it flags anything the notice forgot to say instead of guessing.

                                          How the AI works

                                          A small Flask server sends the notice to Gemma 4 (Google's open model, weights licensed under Apache 2.0) through Google's hosted Gemini API. The model returns structured JSON, which the page renders. Every fact carries the exact words from the notice so you can check it.

                                          Honest limits

                                          AI can make mistakes; always confirm important details with the issuing office. Hostel data in this demo is clearly labelled sample data. The app's own code is MIT-licensed; the hosted API is governed by Google's terms.
